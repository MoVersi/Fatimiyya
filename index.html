Beyond her name, discover her legacy

How well do we really know Lady Fatimah al‑Zahra (SA)?

Begin

We know her as

  1. Daughterof the Messenger of Allah ﷺ
  2. Wifeof Imam Ali (AS)
  3. Motherof Imam Hasan (AS),
    Imam Husayn (AS),
    Lady Zaynab (SA),
    Lady Umm Kulthoom (SA)
    and Mohsin (AS), martyred before he was born

But Lady Fatimah (SA) was much more than these relationships.

In the teachings preserved by the Ahlulbayt (AS), she emerges as a woman of extraordinary

  • knowledge
  • worship
  • purity
  • courage
  • spiritual consciousness

Imam al-Baqir (AS) is reported in al-Kafi to have described Lady Fatimah (SA) in connection with knowledge. It is a reminder that her greatness was not merely inherited through lineage, but reflected in her profound spiritual and intellectual position.1

Her life

  • WorshipHer life was a life of worship and devotion.
  • HomeHer home became a school of faith.
  • WordsHer words became a source of guidance.
  • CourageHer courage became a lesson in standing for truth.
  • NearnessAnd her relationship with Allah became an example for those who seek closeness to Him.

This is why Fatimiyya should not simply be a time to remember that Lady Fatimah (SA) lived.

It should be a time to ask:

  • Do we know what she stood for?
  • Do we understand the depth of her worship?
  • Do we appreciate her knowledge?
  • Do we recognise her courage?
  • Do we understand why the Ahlulbayt (AS) continue to speak of her with such profound reverence?

The Shia tradition preserves an extensive body of narrations about her life and virtues, including in Bihar al-Anwar, which dedicates substantial material to Sayyidah Fatimah al-Zahra (SA).2

Khutbah

Sermon of Fadak

Glory be to Allah for His great blessings, and gratitude to Him for that which He has inspired, and praise be to Him for what He has brought to pass. From the all-encompassing blessings that He grants to the ever-expansive grace that He bestows and all the bounties that He continuously confers. Its magnitude is greater than can be enumerated, its extent is beyond measure and its limit is beyond comprehension. He has invited the people to express their gratitude in order to seek its increase and continuity, called upon them to praise Him by augmenting it and followed up by bidding them to ask for more of its like [in the Hereafter].

I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, alone, having no partners — a statement the inner meaning of which has been defined as sincerity, its means have been instilled in the hearts and its meaning gleams in the minds. He cannot be perceived by sight, described by words or depicted by imaginations. He originated everything from nothing that existed before, and created everything without following any previous model. He fashioned everything with His power and made it all with His will, without any need for bringing it into being or any possible benefit that could come from forming it; only the affirmation of His wisdom, the proclamation of His obedience, the manifestation of His power, the servility of His subjects and the exaltation of His call. Then He placed reward in obedience and punishment in disobedience to Him, in order to turn His servants away from His chastisement and urge them towards His Paradise.

Fatimiyya is therefore not only about mourning her loss.

It is about knowing Lady Fatimah (SA).

  • Knowing the woman behind the name.
  • Understanding the values behind her life.
  • Learning from the daughter of Rasulullah ﷺ.
  • And allowing her example to transform the way we live our own faith.
